What needs attention?

A flag is a question, not a verdict.

Two similar payments may both be valid. Check the invoice or source record before deciding. This app reviews records; it does not move money.

The review rulebook

01 / Repeated reference

Two paid records with the same reference and currency are flagged, even if their amounts differ.

02 / Similar payment

Same payee, amount and currency within two calendar days. Case and extra spaces are ignored; different currencies are never combined.

Your first review, in 60 seconds

1Click Try sample data. You’ll see 12 payments and 6 flagged rows.

2Open PAY-101. PAY-103 has the same invoice reference. These are two flagged records, not two proven losses.

3Choose Needs investigation, write a note, and save. Watch review progress update.

4Export the review. The CSV includes the rule, related IDs, decision and your note.

Import format: id,date,payee,reference,amount,currency,status. Use YYYY-MM-DD dates, positive amounts, three-letter currencies, and paid/pending/failed status. Up to 5,000 rows and 1 MB. A new file or sample replaces the current working set.
